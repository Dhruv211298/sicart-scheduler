⚖️ QA & QC Scheduling Policy (Strict Laboratory Compliance)
  • Single Responsibility: Personnel assigned to QA or QC on any working day must have only one responsibility (no secondary instrument or duty).
  • Consecutive QC → QA Cycle: If an officer is assigned QC activity on a working day, they must be assigned QA activity on the subsequent working day.
  • Single Active Officer: Across the entire laboratory, exactly one officer is active on QA/QC duty on any given working day.
